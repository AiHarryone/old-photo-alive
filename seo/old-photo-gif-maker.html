Why a GIF?

A GIF is the most shareable video format there is: it plays everywhere — messaging apps, social media, email, memorial pages — with no player, no log-in, no clicking “play.” An animated old photo as a GIF lets a memory move wherever you send it.

  4. Save the GIF and share it anywhere that accepts images.

GIFs That Look Right

  • Keep the motion slow — a 3–4 second slow push-in reads as emotional; a fast zoom reads as a tech demo.
  • One clear subject — the eye follows what moves; give it one thing to follow.
  • Start from a good scan — GIF compresses in flat color areas, so a sharp, high-contrast scan exports cleaner.
  • Match the mood to the motion — push-in for portraits, pan across for landscapes, fade for endings.
