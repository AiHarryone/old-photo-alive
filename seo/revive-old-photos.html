Photos That Revive Best

  • One clear face, looking at the camera — a large, well-lit portrait is ideal.
  • Face roughly a third of the frame — too small and detection struggles, too close and it crops oddly.
  • Good contrast — old faded prints still work, but a sharper scan of the print beats a photo of it.
  • No heavy shadows over the eyes — the blink animation needs to see the eyes.
