Why Do It Locally

Background removal is exactly the task you don’t want to upload — portraits, ID documents, product shots. PixelFix runs the person segmentation model in your browser (243KB, on-device), so the photo never leaves your device.

How to Remove a Background

  1. Open PixelFix Remove Background and drop in your photo.
  2. Wait ~1 second — the model detects the person and cuts them out.
  3. Fine-tune with the feather and edge sliders.
  4. Export as a transparent PNG or place it on a white background.
