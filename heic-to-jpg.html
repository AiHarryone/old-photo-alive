HEIC to JPG — common questions

What is HEIC? HEIC is the default photo format on iPhones. Many websites, printers and Windows apps can't read it, which is why you need to convert to JPG.

Is this really free? Yes — the first 100 photos are free forever. After that, unlimited conversion is a one-time $9.99 unlock for the whole PixelFix suite.

Can I convert many at once? Yes — select hundreds of HEIC files and convert them all in one batch, then download as a ZIP.

Are my photos uploaded? No. Everything is decoded locally in your browser using WebAssembly — your photos never leave your device.

HEIC decoding runs in your browser via libheif (WebAssembly). The first conversion loads the ~1MB decoder; after that it's instant.
