FAQ

Is my photo uploaded anywhere? No. Restoration and animation both run in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.

What does Restore do? One-click Auto Fix brings back faded tones, removes colour casts, sharpens soft scans and reduces noise. Fine sliders are available too, and the result feeds into Animate and Alive.

How do I use Alive? Upload a clear, face-forward photo (face at least a third of the frame). Once the face is detected the Alive button appears — click it to see blink, smile and breath.
