## Tips that make old photos look *good* animated

The animation is the easy part. What makes it feel like a memory:

- **Use the highest-resolution scan you have.** A 1000px+ scan animates much more smoothly than a phone photo of a print.
- **Pick a photo with a clear subject.** A face, a couple, a landmark. Busy group shots don't animate as well.
- **Slow is emotional, fast is novelty.** For memorials and tributes, a 3-4 second slow push-in beats a quick zoom every time.
- **Crop before you animate.** Most of the "magic" is choosing *what* the camera slowly moves toward. Get in close on the subject in your head first.
